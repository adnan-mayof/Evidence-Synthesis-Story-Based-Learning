# **Maya's Evidence Synthesis Journey**

> This is a story-based learning project where Maya learns how to conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis with guidance from her mentor.

* **Maya** = the learner
* **Mentor** = the guide
* **Journey** = step-by-step learning
* **Evidence synthesis** = what she is learning

## 👩‍🔬 Maya Has a Question

Maya wants to find out whether **AI-powered learning technologies improve student learning**.

But she doesn't know how to conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Fortunately, Maya has a mentor.

Her mentor doesn't simply give her the answers. Instead, they guide Maya through a real evidence-synthesis project, one step at a time.

Together, Maya and her mentor will work through the questions, decisions, challenges, and research tasks involved in conducting an evidence synthesis.
